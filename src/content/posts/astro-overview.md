Our paper, **“SACO: Sequence-Aware Constrained Optimization Framework for Coupon Distribution in E-commerce,”** has been accepted at AAAI 2026.

The work studies constrained coupon distribution in e-commerce and proposes a sequence-aware optimization framework for large-scale allocation decisions.
