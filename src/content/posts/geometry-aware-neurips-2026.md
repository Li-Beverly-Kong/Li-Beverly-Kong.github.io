Our paper, **“Geometry-Aware Online Scheduling for LLM Serving: From Theoretical Bound to System Practice,”** has been accepted at **NeurIPS 2026**.

The work introduces geometry-aware scheduling algorithms for the dynamic KV-cache footprints of LLM inference, connects them to rigorous theoretical guarantees, and demonstrates practical gains through a plug-and-play implementation for vLLM.

[Read the paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22327).
